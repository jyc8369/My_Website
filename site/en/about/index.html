A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hey, I’m jyc8369.

I enjoy understanding how things work, making things in my own way, and sharing what I learn along the way.

Things I Enjoy

I’m drawn to backend development, infrastructure, and self-hosting. I like following a request through a system, seeing where data goes, and learning what keeps a service running.

I also enjoy building small tools and experiments. Making something myself is a practical way to turn curiosity into understanding.

How I Think

I learn best when I can take ownership of a problem and work through it hands-on. Having room to make decisions, try an approach, and adjust when it doesn’t work helps me understand the whole system.

I value autonomy and responsibility together: the freedom to choose a direction, and the care to see it through.

Why This Site Exists

I picked up the jyc8369.dev domain on a whim. After that, setting up a VPS and putting a site online felt like a good excuse to learn by doing.

It grew into a place to keep projects, notes, and the occasional lesson from things that didn’t go as planned. I’m still figuring out what it will become, and that’s part of the fun.
