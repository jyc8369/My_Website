A Little About Me

A few things I enjoy, what matters to me, and how this site came about.

Things I Enjoy

Hey, I'm jyc8369. I enjoy writing software, working with backend systems and APIs, and running my own services. I also like building PCs and tinkering with electronics.

I like figuring out how things work, whether that's an API, a server, or something on my desk that needs a little tinkering.

How I Think

I like having control over the systems I use. Running things myself lets me understand how they work and make my own decisions. It isn't always the easiest option, but I enjoy figuring things out along the way.

I also prefer costs I can predict and control.

Why This Site Exists

I bought a domain and got a VPS on impulse, without much of a plan. Eventually, I figured I might as well make a little place of my own on the internet. So here it is.
