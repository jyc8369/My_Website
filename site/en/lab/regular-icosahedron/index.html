Regular Icosahedron

Rotate and explore a solid made from 20 equilateral triangles, rendered right here in your browser.

12 VERTICES · 30 EDGES · 20 FACES

CONTROLS

Drag to rotate; use the wheel or zoom control to resize. Focus the viewer and use the arrow keys to rotate with a keyboard.

ABOUT THIS SHAPE

A regular icosahedron is a Platonic solid with 20 identical equilateral-triangle faces. This view defines its vertices with golden-ratio coordinates and draws them with perspective projection on Canvas 2D.
