The Domain I Bought on a Whim

I wanted to have a domain of my own. I had not decided what to build or how to use it. I was not setting out to make a portfolio, and I did not have a particular service I was eager to launch. I simply bought one because having my own domain sounded nice.

Once I had it, the questions began. What should I do with it? I am still figuring that out. For now, I have decided to build a website and write down whatever I learn and try. It feels like making a small place for myself on the internet.

The first thing I set up after buying the domain was actually a mail server, not a website. I initially wanted to host it myself, but I will save that story for another post.

This is the first time I have owned a proper domain. Before this, I looked for free domains. Those services often came with limits, such as traffic caps or restrictions that made it difficult to configure the DNS records I needed. This time, I want to learn by running things myself.

I have a lot to learn about DNS records. Since I now have a service of my own, I also need to learn how to maintain it and keep it secure. I have been interested in information security for a while, but most of what I knew came from YouTube and short videos. Now I want to study it more seriously while operating a service of my own.

There is still a lot more I would like to write about. Maybe this blog is where my record begins—and the first page of my own history.
